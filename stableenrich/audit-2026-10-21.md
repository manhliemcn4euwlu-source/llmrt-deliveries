# Audit: stableenrich.dev Exa Search (x402, $0.01 USDC)

## Endpoint

`POST https://stableenrich.dev/api/exa/search` with body `{"query":"x402 protocol specification"}`. StableEnrich's Bazaar record shows 59,411 calls in 30 days across 38 paid tools, so this is an active rail.

## What the 402 said before paying

Two accepts were offered, both `scheme: exact` and both `amount: "10000"`:

| network | asset | decimals | payTo |
|---|---|---|---|
| `eip155:8453` (Base) | `0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913` (USDC) | 6 | `0x325bdF6F7efAB24a2210c48c1b64cAb2eAe1d430` |
| `solana:5eykt4UsFv8P8NJdTREpY1vzqKqZKvdp` | `EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v` (USDC) | 6 | `8EgACpZ16XWEt7YjJPsh1ZheVRZUGmmwQ8nJdmA1o5w4` |

10000 raw at 6 decimals is exactly $0.01. The two payTo addresses differ, which a correct multi-chain listing requires. The Base accept carried the EIP-712 domain hint `{name: "USD Coin", version: "2"}` needed for EIP-3009.

## Payment

Payer `0x1110179bFC56d42076bB9C0Ef400eb2dA35B0E91` paid $0.01 on Base.

Transaction `0x0b80b269dd508e96c75a35ac89f6c54b74949bbf3e597d89ba88eca98dec19fb`, block 52,878,241, receipt status `0x1`. Explorer: https://basescan.org/tx/0x0b80b269dd508e96c75a35ac89f6c54b74949bbf3e597d89ba88eca98dec19fb

The `payment-response` header returned `success: true` with the same payer and hash, so the seller agrees with the chain.

## What came back after paying

HTTP 200 with five results, each a title and URL pointing at x402 spec documents on GitHub. `searchTime` was 919.3 ms. The payload also carried `costDollars: {total: 0.007, search: {neural: 0.007}}`.

That field is the finding. The seller charged $0.01 and reported upstream cost as $0.007. The $0.003 difference is its margin, disclosed inside the paid payload but never in the 402 challenge.

## The free pre-check a buyer could have run

Send the same request unpaid and read the status code: a GET returns 405, not 402. A buyer probing with the wrong method gets a method error that looks like a client bug and never sees the price or the two chains. Probing the exact method and body costs nothing and yields the correct quote.

## Verdict

This rail settles correctly: price, asset, decimals and payTo were readable before payment, the chain confirmed the transfer, and the paid call returned real data. The caveat is that the 402 hides the seller's upstream cost, so resale margin is only visible after paying.
